01 / THE PLACE

A city shaped
by history.

Vijayapura, formerly known as Bijapur, is one of northern Karnataka's most remarkable heritage cities.

Its story reaches back to the Kalyani Chalukyas and grew into a celebrated centre of art, music and architecture under the Adil Shahi rulers. Magnificent domes, graceful arches, royal palaces, mosques, tombs and fortifications give the city its distinctive character.

Vijayapura means “City of Victory” — a fitting name for a place whose history continues to inspire.

02 / THE HERITAGE TRAIL

Landmarks of
another era.

Explore some of Vijayapura's best-known historical monuments and heritage sites.

01
THE ICONIC DOME

Gol Gumbaz

The grand mausoleum of Muhammad Adil Shah is famous for its enormous dome and remarkable Whispering Gallery, where even a soft sound can travel around the gallery.

02
ROYAL ELEGANCE

Ibrahim Rauza

A beautiful complex of a tomb and mosque, admired for its symmetry, delicate stonework and graceful minarets. It is often called an inspiration for the Taj Mahal.

03
UNFINISHED GRANDEUR

Bara Kaman

The unfinished mausoleum of Ali Adil Shah II, known for its dramatic arches and the striking scale of its original design.

04
ROYAL COURT

Gagan Mahal

A palace and royal audience hall associated with Ali Adil Shah I, recognised for its imposing central arch and historic atmosphere.

05
A GIANT FROM THE PAST

Malik-e-Maidan

A celebrated medieval cannon placed on the fort bastion. Its muzzle is shaped like a lion devouring an elephant.

06
ORNATE GATEWAY

Mehtar Mahal

An ornamental gateway with intricate decorative details, leading towards a mosque and garden.

07
CITY WATCHTOWER

Upli Buruj (Upali Burj)

A tall 16th-century watchtower built as part of Vijayapura's historic defensive system.

08
HISTORIC WATERWORK

Taj Bawdi

A large stepwell built in memory of Queen Taj Sultana, showing the importance of water architecture in the old city.

09
SACRED HERITAGE

Jama Masjid

One of the city's most significant historic mosques, known for its spacious prayer hall and elegant arches.

10
ROYAL RESIDENCE

Asar Mahal

A 17th-century structure traditionally associated with the Adil Shahi court and known for its distinctive setting.

11
FORTIFIED CITY

Vijayapura Fort

The historic fort and citadel area, with massive walls, gateways and bastions that tell the story of the city's military past.

12
ROYAL MONUMENT

Jod Gumbaz

A pair of domed tombs and an important part of the city's wider collection of historic funerary architecture.

13
PALACE REMAINS

Anand Mahal

A two-storeyed palace attributed to Ibrahim Adil Shah II, remembered for its royal architecture and open platform.

14
HISTORIC WATERWORK

Chand Bawdi

An old stepwell that reflects the engineering and water-management traditions of historic Vijayapura.

15
SPIRITUAL LANDMARK

Siddeshwara Temple

A well-known Shiva temple that remains an important place of worship for residents and visitors.

16
A MODERN LANDMARK

Shivagiri

A prominent site featuring a large statue of Lord Shiva and a recognisable landmark in the Vijayapura area.

03 / PLAN YOUR VISIT

Come for the
history. Stay for the stories.

The cooler months from October to March are generally a comfortable time to explore Vijayapura's outdoor heritage sites. Start early, carry water, and give yourself time to admire the details in the stonework.

Respect local customs at places of worship and check current access information before visiting individual monuments.
