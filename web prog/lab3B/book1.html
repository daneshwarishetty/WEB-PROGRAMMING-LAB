About the book

The Alchemist follows Santiago, a young shepherd who travels from Spain to the Egyptian desert in search of a hidden treasure. Along the way, he learns to listen to his heart and follow his dreams.

Author: Paulo Coelho

Genre: Adventure / Philosophical fiction

Language: Originally Portuguese
